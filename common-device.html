Attendance timing rules

  • Clock-in from 5 minutes before through 15 minutes after roster start is on time.
  • Clock-out before scheduled end is early checkout, even by one second.
  • Clock-out from scheduled end through 15 minutes after is on time.
  • Late arrival / early departure is flagged for review; no silent time alteration.
